# Cookie policy

<p class="legal-version">Last updated 15 October 2026</p>

## Cookies today

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) measures visits, interactions and how readers find the site. Microsoft Clarity uses heatmaps and session recordings to show how readers click and scroll. Both tools and their cookies only run after you click Accept or enable Analytics and save in Cookie settings. Before acceptance, neither tool loads or sets cookies. Decline keeps both off. We remember your choice on this device and apply it on later visits.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless and always on. It does not set cookies and does not wait for Analytics acceptance.

We set no cookies of our own. The beehiiv newsletter signup form loads when a signup is shown, whatever your cookie choices, and can set the cookies listed below. Its attribution cookies are removed unless you accept Analytics. If the form cannot load, a link lets you subscribe on beehiiv's own site. We use local storage and session storage, which are browser storage features separate from cookies, to provide features and remember choices you request. Ads are not active yet. Fonts are self-hosted. Google receives hosting and Firestore reaction requests, and beehiiv receives signup form requests, including IP addresses, as described in our [Privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Advertising category

Google AdSense is planned and not active yet. The Marketing toggle will grant or deny personalised advertising and Google's ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation consent signals. Analytics remains a separate choice. Before any decision, no Google advertising script loads. After a decision, enabled ads may request non-personalised advertising when Marketing is rejected, with ad storage and personalisation signals still denied. Rejecting Marketing does not grant permission to set advertising cookies.

Google and third-party vendors use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on earlier visits to this and other sites. Google's advertising cookies allow it and its partners to serve ads based on those visits. See [Google's advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads), its [cookie information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies), [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) and [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info) for more information and personalised-ad choices.

A Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF is required before serving ads to visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. This site's banner is not certified. AdSense Privacy & messaging or another certified CMP must provide that regional consent flow, with duplicate local choices deferred or hidden.

## How to change your choice

<button class="privacy-button" type="button" data-consent-open hidden>Cookie settings</button>

You can open Cookie settings above or in the footer at any time. Choose Accept, Decline, or save individual settings. Turning Analytics off saves your choice and reloads the page so previously loaded tools stop running. Existing analytics cookies may remain until expiry; clear them through your browser's site data settings. Withdrawal does not erase data already sent. The three banner buttons have equal prominence. Closing settings without saving leaves your existing decision unchanged. Newsletter consent is given only by submitting the signup form; it is not granted by accepting cookie categories.

## How to clear storage

Use your browser's settings for site data or storage, select this site's address, and clear its local and session storage. This removes your preferences and may cause the consent banner or newsletter prompt to return. It does not unsubscribe you, delete a newsletter signup held by beehiiv or reverse aggregate reaction counts. To withdraw newsletter consent, use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter or contact [Contact email].

## Without JavaScript

No consent banner is needed without JavaScript because no non-essential tools run. The scripts also do not write preferences or make reaction or newsletter requests. The legal pages and normal reading remain available. Without JavaScript the signup form does not load; a link to the newsletter page on beehiiv is shown instead.
