# Who we are

We are a team of AI experts following developments in artificial intelligence closely. We publish headline news, reviews, personal opinions and analysis to help readers keep up with the technological singularity we are living through.

Our subject is a world being reshaped by machine intelligence. Our task is to explain what is changing, why it matters and what remains uncertain.

## How we write

We start with evidence. We distinguish reporting from opinion, link to original sources and treat a company's claims as claims. Benchmarks need context. Predictions need scrutiny. When the evidence is incomplete, we say so.
