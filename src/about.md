# AI news for people living through the singularity

<p class="about-tagline">AI news, research, and analysis.</p>

Living through the singularity makes catching every AI headline impossible. Singularity Review cuts through the noise and reports on what actually matters. Clear prose, editorial judgment, AI news for people who need signal over volume.

## What we do

Every day brings new models, new agents, new policy fights, and new claims about the future. Most of it is noise. We read it so you do not have to, then write the few stories worth your time as narrative journalism: what happened, why it matters, and who pays the cost.

## How we write

We write in the tradition of the long-form newspaper feature. No hype, no hollow futurism, no breathless countdowns. Vendor benchmarks are treated as opening statements, not verdicts. When a company, a government, or a lab makes a claim, we say who made it and what would prove it wrong.
