# About

<p class="legal-version">Last updated 11 October 2026</p>


Singularity Review covers AI news, research and policy. The publication includes reporting, reviews, personal opinions and analysis about the technological singularity, the idea that advancing intelligence could accelerate technological change.

## What we cover

We follow new AI models, the systems trained to produce responses or solve tasks, and agents, systems that use tools to carry out work. We also cover research into their capabilities and risks, and the policy decisions that shape how they are used.
