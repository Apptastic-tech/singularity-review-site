# Contact

<p class="legal-version">Last updated 11 October 2026</p>

## General enquiries

Singularity Review is operated by [Company legal name]. Write to [Contact email] with questions about the publication, a story suggestion or a request concerning our content. Put the subject of your enquiry in the message and include relevant article links so it can reach the appropriate person.

## Corrections

Send correction requests to [Contact email]. Include the article URL, the passage you believe is wrong, a brief explanation and any source that supports the correction. We review requests on their merits and may ask for more information. Our [corrections policy in Editorial standards](/editorial-standards/#corrections) explains how substantive changes should be recorded.

## Privacy requests

Use [Contact email] for questions about personal data or to exercise the rights described in our [Privacy policy](/privacy/). For newsletter requests, identify the email address you used. Please do not send identity documents or other sensitive information unless we ask for information needed to verify a request. Cookie choices can be changed through Cookie settings in the footer.

## Advertising enquiries

Send advertising enquiries to [Contact email]. Describe your organisation, the proposed campaign and any relevant dates. Advertising arrangements are separate from editorial decisions. Enquiries do not guarantee placement, coverage or a response by a particular date. Ads are not active on the site yet.
